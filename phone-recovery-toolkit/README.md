# Quick Phone Recovery

A simple, static, computer-assisted guide for recovering access to phones you own or are authorized to manage.

## Quick start on a Windows PC

1. Open [the guide on GitHub](https://github.com/mallinpaul848-coder/ARIES-Tier0/blob/main/phone-recovery-toolkit/index.html).
2. Choose the exact message displayed by your phone.
3. Follow the short checklist and open the official support link for your device.
4. For an iPhone passcode reset, use Apple's Apple Devices app on Windows and follow Apple's recovery-mode restore instructions.

The page is an instruction guide only. It does not automatically detect the connected phone, communicate over USB, or unlock the device.

## What it covers

- iPhone forgotten passcode: official computer restore route (erases the phone)
- iPhone Activation Lock: linked Apple Account or official ownership review
- Android forgotten screen PIN/pattern: manufacturer-specific support route
- Google Factory Reset Protection (FRP): recovery using an account previously synced to the device
- Samsung Galaxy device protection
- Carrier/SIM network unlocking
- Company/school mobile-device management release

## Important limitations

There is no universal, legitimate USB tool that instantly unlocks every phone. A computer restore can erase data and does not remove Apple Activation Lock, Google FRP, or server-side mobile-device management. Those protections require the linked account, carrier approval, an authorized administrator, or manufacturer ownership review. Avoid paid tools claiming to bypass these protections.

Never enter device passwords, passcodes, IMEI/serial numbers, or proof-of-purchase documents into this guide. Share ownership documents only directly with the official provider when required.
